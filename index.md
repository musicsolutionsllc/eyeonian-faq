# EyeOnian™ Frequently Asked Questions

## Where are my EyeOnotes stored? And are the stored EyeOnotes shared across platforms?

Your EyeOnotes are saved wherever you created them — on the device you were using, or in the browser you were using. They are **not** shared between platforms. An EyeOnote you save in the Android app will not appear in the web version or on your iPad, and one you save on your iPad will not appear on Android or the web. Each platform keeps its own set.

Where a version does offer cloud storage, it uses **your own** personal cloud account, and it keeps your EyeOnotes available across the devices or browsers signed in to that same account — not across different platforms. Music Solutions LLC does not store your EyeOnotes and cannot read them.

To move a single EyeOnote from one platform to another, share it: create a share link from the EyeOnote and open that link on the other device.

### iPad / macOS

Your EyeOnotes are stored on the device and synced through **iCloud**, in your own private iCloud storage. They are available on your other Apple devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, your EyeOnotes are still saved — they simply stay on that one device until you sign in.

### Android

Your EyeOnotes are stored **on the device only**. The Android app has no cloud storage and makes no network connection for your saved work.

If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google Account, your EyeOnotes may be included in the device backup that Android makes and restored when you reinstall the app. That is a feature of the Android system rather than part of EyeOnian™, it is controlled in your device settings, and a restore happens only at the moment the app is installed.

### Web

Your EyeOnotes are stored **in the browser** on the computer you are using, so by default they are available on that browser and computer.

You can also connect **OneDrive** from the app's settings. When you do, your EyeOnotes are stored in a folder the app creates inside your own OneDrive account, and they become available in the other browsers and computers where you sign in. This is optional and off until you choose to connect it.
